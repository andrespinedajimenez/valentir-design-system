# Logos

**Vector (SVG, transparent background)** — traced from the official PNGs and filled with the exact `logo-teal`, `logo-orange`, `logo-ink` and `logo-slate` colors. The preferred version for web, print and presentations:

- `valentir-logo-horizontal.svg` — primary lockup in color.
- `valentir-logo-stacked-tagline.svg` — stacked lockup with tagline in color.
- `valentir-logo-horizontal-white.svg` and `valentir-logo-stacked-tagline-white.svg` — negative versions for dark backgrounds (`surface-inverse`, `green-900`, scrimmed photos): all white except the `logo-orange` dot.

These are an automatic trace, not the original artwork: at billboard size or in close-up a few edges may look slightly uneven (e.g. the “r” in Green and the thin tagline letters). Have a designer clean them up before large-format print.

**Original files supplied by the brand** (PNG/JPG on white):

- `valentir-logo-horizontal.png` — primary lockup. Mark on the left, “Valentir” in `logo-ink` and letter-spaced “Green Tech” in `logo-slate`. 1026×414.
- `valentir-logo-stacked-tagline.png` — stacked lockup with a teal rule and the tagline “SMARTER FLEETS. SAFER OPERATIONS. GREENER FUTURE.” in `logo-slate`. 684×887.
- `valentir-logo-sheet.png` — presentation sheet with both lockups side by side; reference only, not for placement.
- `valentir-mark.webp` — the mark alone, 80×80 on transparent (from the site). Show at 40px in the header and 36px in the footer, and as an avatar.
- `valentir-favicon.png` — 32×32 favicon.
- `valentir-logo-horizontal-alt.jpg` — horizontal variant with more saturated colors (teal ≈ #007c72, orange ≈ #fc5e02). **Do not use** until the brand confirms which palette is current; the official one is `logo-teal` / `logo-orange`.

Rules: don't redraw, recolor, stretch or rotate the logo; keep clear space equal to the orange dot’s height on every side; don't place it on photography. On dark backgrounds use the `-white.svg` versions.
