# Hero

Full-bleed page header: fleet photography under the 90° `scrim`, a `teal-600` eyebrow, a `hero-title` H1 and two CTAs.

**What the consumer provides:** a landscape photo (≥1440×420) with the subject in the right half and a descriptive `alt`, the eyebrow (usually the brand or product-line name), a 3–5 word headline, a one-sentence intro and two actions.

- Minimum height 420px; `space-4xl` vertical padding. Text aligns to the 1200px container.
- The scrim is mandatory: without it white text fails contrast over the photo.
- Do: short, concrete headlines (“Clarity for every mile”). Don't: more than two buttons, text over the right half of the image, photos with no truck or data.
