# SiteFooter

Footer on `surface-inverse` with a 3px `green-900` top rule: the negative horizontal logo (`valentir-logo-horizontal-white.svg`, 36px tall), round social icons and four columns (Products, Solutions, Company, Legal).

**What the consumer provides:** each column’s routes and the social profiles (valentirtech on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram).

- Headings in `footer-heading` (UPPERCASE), links in `caption` at 0.9 opacity, legal in `legal` `ink-inverse-tertiary`.
- Always include `llms.txt` under Legal: it’s part of the site’s AI-search visibility strategy.
- Don't: forms inside the footer (the newsletter comes before it, in its own panel).
