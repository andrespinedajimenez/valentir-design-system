# CookieBanner

Floating consent notice at the bottom left: `surface` with `elevation-8`, `radius-md`, max width 420px.

**What the consumer provides:** the legal text (which cookies and why), the policy link and the Accept / Decline handlers.

- Accept is `vg-btn--primary`; Decline is outlined — both choices must be equally easy to make.
- Don't: block the page, hide Decline or add a “Settings” button without a real panel.
